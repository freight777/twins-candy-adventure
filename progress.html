iPad tips

  • Lock the screen in landscape: swipe down from the top-right corner and tap the rotation lock.
  • Keep a child inside one game: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, then triple-click the top button in the game.
  • Grown-up buttons in the games (the house, this page, the candy menu corner) open only when held for a moment, so mashing can't open them.
