## Review: found and fixed

Status: `[x]` done and deployed, `[ ]` not done yet.

- [x] **Sparkle Match ran off the side of a phone held upright.** The board's last column (and the goal card) were cut off by
      about 20 px on 375–390 px phones: the play screen's layout let the goal bar widen the board's box past the screen.
      Fixed; the nav test now checks the whole board is on screen at five phone and tablet sizes, and fails without the fix.
- [x] **Book covers crowded on narrower shelves** (tablet upright, phone sideways): pictures overflowed and titles slid under the
      page-count ribbon. Each cover now sizes its picture and title to its own width.
- [x] **Empty album slots were blank.** Each sticker still to make shows as a faint shadow of its picture, so he can see what's
      left to collect, and tapping any slot says its name (an empty slot used to do nothing when tapped).

- [x] **Honeycomb mazes too small on an upright phone.** About 28 px a corridor, under the spec's 30 px rule: the size limit
      forgot the honeycomb's extra half column. Fixed; the maze test now also checks phone-sized and short landscape boxes.
- [x] **Score Spy's cover said "Game Day".** It now says Score Spy, and its trophy wears a medal so it isn't a copy of the
      Game Day trophy.
- [x] **A lonely sixth book on its own row.** The hidden-pictures and sticker planks hold six books now (five themes plus Score
      Spy or the Album); covers size so six fit on a tablet in either direction.
- [x] **Score Spy's clue card did nothing when tapped** (only the small ▶ did). The whole card says the clue again.
- [x] **Sparkle Match map: level stones overlapped where the path turns** (2 and 3, 5 and 6, 8 and 9 were 60 px apart but
      66 px wide). They are 72 px apart now.
- [x] **Printing "a 10-page book" always printed pages 1–10**, the easiest ones and often already done. It prints the next ten
      pages he hasn't finished, at the levels the game has for him.
- [x] **Real scores: two teams with the same name** (New York Giants and San Francisco Giants) were dropped, or only one got its
      city. Both now get full names. Found by the new real-scores browser test.
